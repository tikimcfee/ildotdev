entry

A list item — project, writing piece, anything that wants a title, optional date, and a one-line gloss. The whole card is the link. Stacks with a small gap between items.

2026-04

title goes here

a one-line description, set in italic dim.

a project entry

the meta line is optional — drop it if there's no date.

 
<a class="entry" href="...">
  <div class="meta">2026-04</div>       <!-- optional -->
  <h3>title</h3>
  <p class="gloss">one-line description.</p>
</a>

provenance

Tells the reader who wrote a piece. Add a .by chip inside an .entry (it floats to the top-right), and/or a .provenance line on the content itself. Three states: .by-human, .by-ai, .by-mixed. Only for content with a voice — leave nav/factual cards untagged.

ai
2026-05

a generated piece

research notes, a survey, anything machine-written.

 human

a piece you wrote

your words, your voice.

 human + ai

a collaboration

a draft you revised, or your reply to a generated piece.

  <p class="gloss">…</p>
</a>
